# Small Business Document Toolkit: Checklists for Freelancers, Landlords and Offices

Free checklists for the business paperwork that causes the most disputes and
month-end headaches: closing out a tenancy and returning a security deposit,
running a petty cash float that actually reconciles, and keeping the right
documents for every freelance job. Each checklist is short, ordered, and written
to be used while you work — not read once and forgotten.

Companion toolkit for [Kitovo](https://www.kitovo.app), a free receipt generator
that runs entirely in your browser.

## Who this is for

- **Landlords** returning a security deposit and documenting deductions fairly
- **Freelancers** who want a complete paper trail for every client payment
- **Office managers and small teams** who handle petty cash and expense slips

## Frequently asked questions

**How is a security deposit refund calculated?**
Refund = deposit held − itemised deductions (unpaid rent, cleaning, repairs beyond
normal wear). If deductions exceed the deposit, the statement shows a balance owed
instead of a negative refund. See the
[end-of-tenancy checklist](checklists/landlord-end-of-tenancy-checklist.md).

**What makes a petty cash voucher valid?**
Date, amount, purpose, cost code, the vendor's slip, and three different signatures:
Issued by, Received by, Approved by. Without them, the float will not reconcile.

**Which documents should a freelancer keep for each job?**
Four: the quote/proforma, the invoice, the payment receipt, and a proof-of-payment
record matched to the bank transaction. The
[freelancer checklist](checklists/freelancer-document-checklist.md) walks through each.

**What if a receipt or vendor slip is lost?**
Ask the issuer for a duplicate first. If none exists, document the expense honestly
with a missing-receipt declaration and supporting evidence — Kitovo's
[Missing Receipt Declaration Generator](https://www.kitovo.app/lost-receipt-replacement/)
creates that document.
