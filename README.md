# Small Business Document Toolkit

Checklists and worked examples for the paperwork small businesses, freelancers and
landlords actually get wrong: end-of-tenancy deposit returns, petty cash, and the
documents a freelancer should keep for every job. Companion toolkit for
[Kitovo](https://www.kitovo.app), the free receipt generator.

## Checklists

- [Freelancer document checklist](checklists/freelancer-document-checklist.md) — quote, invoice, receipt, proof of payment: what to issue and keep for every client job.
- [Landlord end-of-tenancy checklist](checklists/landlord-end-of-tenancy-checklist.md) — final rent receipt, security deposit return with itemised deductions, and the paper trail both sides need.
- [Petty cash checklist](checklists/petty-cash-checklist.md) — running a small office cash float with vouchers, cost codes and three signatures so it reconciles at month end.

## Responsible use

For documenting **real** transactions only — never fabricate a receipt, voucher or
deposit statement for something that did not happen.
