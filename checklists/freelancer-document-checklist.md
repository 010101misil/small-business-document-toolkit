# Freelancer document checklist

For every client job, keep these four documents. Together they answer the three
questions clients, accountants and tax offices ask: what was agreed, what was charged,
and what was actually paid.

## Before the work
- [ ] Written quote or proforma invoice with scope, price, currency and due date
      → https://www.kitovo.app/proforma-invoice-template/
- [ ] Client's legal name and address (and VAT/tax ID for EU/UK B2B work)

## When you deliver
- [ ] Invoice or bill with a unique number, issue date and itemised lines
- [ ] For EU/UK cross-border B2B services: reverse-charge wording where it applies
      → https://www.kitovo.app/eu-receipt-generator/ · https://www.kitovo.app/uk-receipt-generator/

## When you get paid
- [ ] Receipt for the payment (amount, date, method, job reference)
      → https://www.kitovo.app/freelance-receipt-generator/
- [ ] Proof-of-payment record matched to the bank/card transaction
      → https://www.kitovo.app/proof-of-payment-receipt-template/
- [ ] Partial payment? Record Amount Paid and Balance Due on the same receipt —
      never issue a second "full" receipt later.

## Filing
- [ ] One folder per client per year: quote → invoice → receipt → bank record
- [ ] If a receipt is lost, ask the payer for a duplicate first; if none exists,
      document it honestly: https://www.kitovo.app/lost-receipt-replacement/
